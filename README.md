# LUMIVO AI

Starter frontend for a separate AI creative platform.

Includes:
- Text to Video
- Photo to Video
- Text to Image
- AI Thumbnail
- Multi-language selector
- 4 sec, 8 sec, 10 sec, 30 sec, 1 min, 5 min, 10 min options
- Demo credit system
- Monthly, 6-month and yearly plan UI

Actual AI generation, secure credits and payments require a backend/API.
